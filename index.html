Online Monitoring of Unauthorized Construction Across the City

Project Overview

This platform uses a ChangeFormer (Transformer-based Siamese network) pretrained on the LEVIR-CD dataset to detect structural changes between two satellite / drone images of the same area. Upload your own before & after images, or browse the built-in LEVIR-CD+ test dataset to see change detection in action.

How It Works

  1. Upload Images: Submit an old and a new image of the same area, or pick a pair from the LEVIR-CD+ dataset.
  2. Change Detection: The backend runs the ChangeFormerV6 model to produce a pixel-level change mask.
  3. Highlight Changes: Detected change regions are shown as bounding boxes and an overlay mask on the new image.
  4. Results: The site displays the change percentage and marks whether significant construction change is detected.
