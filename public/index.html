Online Monitoring of Unauthorized Construction Across the City

Project Overview

MCD is working on monitoring unauthorized construction across the city. A viable and low-cost solution is required. Drone-based surveys of swathes of land/areas at defined time intervals will help in real-time detection of unauthorized construction. We plan to train a model for change detection using old and new images in the future. For now, this site will serve as the foundation for our monitoring platform.

How It Works

  1. Upload Images: Submit an old and a new image of the same area, or select a pre-added dataset.
  2. Change Detection: Our backend (demo: simulated) uses a Siamese CNN model to compare the images and generate a change mask.
  3. Highlight Changes: Detected changes are highlighted with bounding boxes on the new image.
  4. Illegal Detection (Demo): Fake parameters or rules are applied (e.g., large change area, specific regions) to mark detected changes as illegal construction for demonstration purposes.
  5. Results: The site displays the bounding boxes and a message indicating whether illegal construction is detected, along with details.

This workflow is designed for demonstration and can be extended with real models and rules for production use.
